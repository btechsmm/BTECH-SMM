Your ambassador dashboard at a glance

Portal Identity

  • Account Status: Visible as "Active" with your profile picture.
  • Ambassador ID: Your unique reference (e.g., BTECH-AMB-000001).
  • Commission Rate: Your current earning percentage on qualifying orders.
  • Referral Code: The unique suffix used to track your invitations.

Note: Terminology and rates are based on the live portal interface provided in this guide.
